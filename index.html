Welcome to Dominion Park!

This is a park near a housing suburb off of Leesville Road. This guide will show you everything you need to know about the park and the area around it.

Facilities

Dominion Park features the following facilities within it:

  1. Playground
  2. Large Grass Field
  3. Swingset
  4. Volleyball Court
  5. Clubhouse and Pool

Nearby Activities

  • Park Walking and Biking Trail
  • Basketball Court
  • Benches and Picnic Tables
